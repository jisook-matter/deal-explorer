For Adam · style notes

What changed, and the tokens to match matter.city

Same content and logic as your version. The changes are layout and styling, so it reads like part of the new website.

Layout

  • All inputs in one panel, including the care tier. Term cards below are a comparison, not a second control.
  • Total and next steps sit together at the top: request a quote, download PDF.
  • One "Year 1 is covered" note instead of the message repeating through the page.
  • Phone: sticky total bar at the bottom so the price stays visible while choosing.

Type and numbers

  • Poppins only. Numbers use tabular figures (font-variant-numeric: tabular-nums) instead of a monospace font.
  • H1 52/700, −2.5% tracking. Card labels 12/600 uppercase, 0.2em tracking.
  • Body 15–17/400 in #4A5054. Muted text #6A7176.
  • Big total 64/700, −3% tracking, white on the dark card.

Colour and shape

Ink #16191B Soft #F6F7F8 Line #E7E9EB Waste #43B02A #205C40 Water #3E8DD6 #175787 Nudge #F19E21
  • Accent follows the market: green for Waste, blue for Water. The CTA stays brand green.
  • Radii 20 (cards), 12 (options), pill buttons. Neutral grey cards instead of green-tinted boxes with borders.
  • Selected state is a 1.5px accent outline. Colour is never the only signal.
